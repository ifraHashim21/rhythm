Poems

Whispers of Dawn
Soft light spills on dewdrop dreams,
As silence hums in golden streams.
Nightfall Sonata
A hush of stars, the moon takes flight,
A lullaby in endless night.
Moonlit Thread
The stars stitch dreams across the sky, In silence, night begins to cry.
Glass Wings
A butterfly lands, then takes the air, Fragile beauty, unaware.
Echo
A single word in canyon thrown, Returns to find itself alone.
Still Rain
Each drop a note on windows sung, A lullaby for old and young.
paper Boat
I sent my hopes on folded stream, They sailed away with every dream.
Ashes
What once was fire, now dust below, Yet warmth remains in afterglow.
Threadbare Sky
The stars look thin, like worn-out cloth, But still they hold the weight of thought.
Hollow Tree
An empty trunk, the forest's ear, It holds the secrets no one hears.
Lantern
A tiny flame in endless black, It never stops, it never looks back.
Quiet Room
No words were said, yet all was known, In silence, love had gently grown.

About Us

Rhythm House is more than just a music store — it's a place where passion for music meets community, nostalgia, and modern trends. Established in the heart of the city, Rhythm House has been a trusted destination for music enthusiasts of all age groups for years.

Our Collection

From the very beginning, our goal has been to create a space where every music lover can find what they're looking for — whether it's a rare tape from the golden era, the latest chart-topping albums, or a soulful devotional collection.

We pride ourselves on our diverse and extensive collection of music across various formats — CDs, DVDs, and tapes. Whether you're a parent looking for poems and rhymes for your children, a teenager searching for the latest pop hits, or an aficionado of 60s and 70s rock, we've got you covered.

Special Features

One of the highlights of Rhythm House is our exceptional collection of motion picture soundtracks, spanning classic films to contemporary cinema. Our rock 'n roll section is one of the largest in the region.

Can't find what you're looking for? Our team is committed to sourcing it for you within a week — a testament to our dedication to customer satisfaction.

More Than Music

Beyond music, we offer a curated range of books and magazines, including sought-after international titles like Guitar Legends and Total Guitar, air-mailed directly from the United States.

Community & Events

We regularly host live shows and music contests, providing a platform for local and emerging artists. Our events are well-planned, inclusive celebrations of musical talent.

Membership Benefits

Join our membership program with purchases of ₹200 or more to enjoy exclusive benefits including:

  • Discounts on purchases
  • Reward points program
  • Early access to events
  • Special offers throughout the year

At Rhythm House, we believe music is not just something you listen to — it's an experience, a memory, a part of your identity. Visit us at rhythmhouse.com or in-store to explore the world of music with us.
